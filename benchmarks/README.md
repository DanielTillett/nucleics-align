# Benchmarks

Scripts for reproducing the timings in [docs/performance.md](../docs/performance.md) and the manuscript ([paper/manuscript.md](../paper/manuscript.md)). Build the tools first with `make` in the repository root; GNU `time` (`/usr/bin/time`) is used for CPU time and peak RSS.

- **`benchmarks/run.sh [lengths]`** — runs `needleman_wunsch` (`full`, `--linear`, `--scoreonly`) and `smith_waterman --maxhits 1` on simulated DNA pairs from `benchmarks/make_pairs.sh` (best of 3 runs).
- **`benchmarks/real_sequences.sh [output_dir] [threads]`** — downloads the seven public NCBI RefSeq/GenBank pairs in Table 1, logs SHA-256 digests, times all modes with pinned threads (default 8), verifies identical global scores across modes, and computes full-alignment identity.
- **`benchmarks/protein_bench.sh [work_dir] [threads]`** — downloads five public NCBI RefSeq protein orthologue pairs (TP53, POLR2A, BRCA1, MTOR, ATM; 390–3,066 aa), logs full SHA-256 digests, and benchmarks original `seq-align` (`dc41988`) against Nucleics Align under BLOSUM62 (`-10 / -1`) with score assertions (Table 5).
- **`benchmarks/gen_pairs.py <length> <div|rand> <seed> [out.fa]`** — deterministic, platform-independent Python 3 DNA pair generator (MT19937 PRNG).
- **`benchmarks/ablation.c`** — C11 library-level fresh-process ablation driver timing nine configurations (scalar 3-matrix, compact 1-byte + striped 32-bit, difference-encoded 8-bit, certified banding with 12-mer vs WFA sizing, multi-threading, linear space, and BiWFA) with exact peak RSS and score assertions (Table 2).
- **`benchmarks/compare_harness.sh [work_dir] [threads] [all|ablation|global|edlib|local]`** — builds pinned revisions of original `seq-align` (`dc41988`), `ksw2` (`289609b`), `WFA2-lib` (`v2.3.5` / `cf3eb92`), `edlib` (`v1.2.7` / `ec2310e`), and `SSW` (`a66636b`), then runs the fresh-process 9-configuration ablation and 5-seed comparator suite (20 kb and 50 kb affine global, 20 kb and 50 kb unit-cost edit distance vs `edlib`, and 20 kb local vs `SSW` with per-seed scores and `ssw_flag` reporting) (Tables 2 and 4).
- **`benchmarks/journal_bench.sh [work_dir] [threads]`** — run after `compare_harness.sh` and `protein_bench.sh` using the same `work_dir`; builds pinned `parasail` (`v2.6.2` / `600fb26`, AVX2 16-bit striped global traceback), runs `parasail` on the five real proteins with independent BLOSUM62 CIGAR/alignment re-scoring (Table 5), independently re-scores cross-tool 20 kb DNA alignments/CIGARs (`full`, `--linear`, `--wfa`, `ksw2`, `WFA2 BiWFA`), and runs the full deterministic 5 kb – 1 Mb scaling suite with matched `WFA2 BiWFA` comparisons at 100 kb – 1 Mb (Table 3).
- **`benchmarks/journal_extra.py`** — benchmarks `parasail` AVX2 `scan` (`nw_trace_scan_avx2_256_16`) on the five real proteins and runs independent CIGAR/alignment re-scoring on deep BiWFA (`100 kb 1%` and `1 Mb 0.1%`).
- **`benchmarks/results/pop-os-v1.3.0.txt` & `pop-os-v1.3.0-journal.txt`** — archived benchmark outputs for Tables 1–5 (Nucleics Align v1.3.0, commit `2529b86`), superseding the v1.2.0 logs (`pop-os-v1.2.0*.txt`), including per-seed wall times, median CPU times and peak RSS, local per-seed scores and SSW flags, independent CIGAR/alignment re-scoring checks, CPU/governor/SMT metadata, full SHA-256 digests for all synthetic/protein FASTAs and Nucleics Align CLI binaries, and 12-character SHA-256 prefixes for Table 1 FASTAs.
